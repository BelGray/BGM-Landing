THE ARTIST

Grounded in classical piano keys, engineered for the energy of electronic music.

Bel Gray is the artist alias of composer, electronic music producer, and pianist Daniel Belyaev. Built upon classical piano discipline and deep DAW engineering in FL Studio, every release is designed as an authentic emotional timeline.

Most works across the catalog are purely handcrafted piano compositions and electronic synth productions. On select concept releases (like Anxious Story), neural voice synthesis and modern technology are orchestrated as creative instruments — where melodic architecture, harmony, and emotional weight always remain human-led.
